# 02 — Elsevier policies that gate acceptance (distilled, rule-id'd)
Sources: generative-ai-policies-for-journals; publishing-ethics (#2 editors, #4 authors);
authorship & changes; competing interests; funding; preprints; inclusive language;
image manipulation; jurisdictional claims; sharing (share link); researcher academy tips.

## Generative AI (POL-AI-*)
- POL-AI-1 AI tools may assist (language, organisation, literature triage) ONLY under human
  oversight; never a substitute for author judgement.
- POL-AI-2 **Authors may not list/cite AI as an author.**
- POL-AI-3 Disclosure mandatory at first submission: new section BEFORE the reference list,
  titled "Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript
  preparation process", wording: "During the preparation of this work the author(s) used
  [TOOL] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and
  edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the
  published article." (basic grammar/spell/reference tools exempt).
- POL-AI-4 Authors accountable for verifying AI output incl. **checking AI-generated
  references for fabrication**; data privacy & IP per tool T&Cs.
- POL-AI-5 Reviewers/editors may NOT upload the unpublished manuscript into GenAI tools
  (supportive use for report wording/background search allowed).

## Authorship & ethics (POL-AU-*)
- POL-AU-1 Authorship = substantial contribution to (1) conception/design/data acquisition/
  analysis, (2) drafting or critical revision, (3) final approval; + accountability for all parts.
- POL-AU-2 One corresponding author; only their affiliation determines publishing-agreement
  eligibility/discounts.
- POL-AU-3 Authorship changes: essentially frozen at submission; editor-approved form only
  before acceptance; **never after acceptance**.
- POL-AU-4 Submission declaration: not published elsewhere (preprint/thesis/abstract OK),
  not under consideration elsewhere, all authors approve; screening tools may check.
- POL-AU-5 Publishing ethics: authors' duties (originality, no data fabrication, no duplicate
  submission, disclose conflicts); editors recuse on own/family/colleague papers.

## Conflicts & funding (POL-COI-*, POL-FUND-*)
- POL-COI-1 Complete the Elsevier **declarations tool**; upload resulting .doc/.docx at
  "attach files"; "I have nothing to declare" if none; journal-role authors add the standard
  non-involvement sentence.
- POL-FUND-1 Disclose all funding with role statement; standard format
  "Funding: This work was supported by … [grant numbers …]"; if none use:
  "This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public,
  commercial, or not-for-profit sectors."

## Preprints & sharing (POL-PRE-*)
- POL-PRE-1 Preprints (incl. SSRN auto-post option at submission) ≠ prior publication;
  corresponding author seeks co-author approval before posting.
- POL-SHR-1 After acceptance: Share Link gives 50 days' free access to the formatted article
  (promotion channel; keep for co-authors & social).

## Language & images (POL-LANG-*, POL-IMG-*)
- POL-LANG-1 Inclusive language: no bias by age/gender/race/ethnicity/culture/orientation/
  disability; plural defaults; avoid he/she; no cultural assumptions.
- POL-IMG-1 No enhancement/obscuring/moving/removing/introducing features; brightness/contrast
  only if no information lost; nonlinear (gamma) adjustments disclosed in legend;
  AI creation/manipulation of primary research images forbidden; image screening may occur.
- POL-MAP-1 Maps: study area only; note "map lines delineate study areas and do not
  necessarily depict accepted national boundaries".

## Editorial duties relevant to authors (POL-ED-*)
- POL-ED-1 Editors decide; recuse on conflicts; appeals once, final.
- POL-ED-2 Desk-reject triggers authors should self-screen: out-of-scope, policy breach,
  missing mandatory items (highlights, graphical abstract, data statement, AI declaration,
  line numbers for ENB), inconsistent referencing, unverifiable citations.
