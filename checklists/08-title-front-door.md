# 08 — Title & front-door review (TTL-*) — dedicated group G1
The title is the desk editor's first scope signal and the search engine's main hook; it gets
its OWN group (3+ agents with different lenses), never merged with abstract/keywords review.

## Rules
- TTL-1 Concise & informative (ENB-TP-1): target ≤15–20 words; every extra clause must earn
  its place with a scope or discoverability gain.
- TTL-2 No abbreviations/formulae unless established (DNA-level fame); "RECS", "LOGO", "R2"
  forbidden in title; spell out or drop.
- TTL-3 Must contain the axis tokens a building-energy editor searches: residential/building
  stock, energy prediction/consumption, national-scale or dataset name spelled out, and the
  trustworthiness angle (leakage audit / uncertainty / uncertainty-calibrated).
- TTL-4 No overclaim (CLM-*): title may not promise what results bound only ("bounds",
  "honest ceiling" ok; "guarantees savings", "solves leakage" not ok).
- TTL-5 Structure: [method/quality phrase] + [task] + [evidence scope] colon-subtitle pattern
  is acceptable; subtitle ≤ one clause; no double colons, no trailing "," or ".".
- TTL-6 Scope fit vs SRC-26/27: title keywords must intersect ENB aims (building energy use,
  stock modelling, performance gap, retrofit) — verify live against insights/call-for-papers.
- TTL-7 Discoverability: first 4–6 words carry SEO weight; put the searchable noun phrase
  first ("Residential energy prediction …" beats "A leakage-audited …" if equal honest).
- TTL-8 Consistency: title ↔ abstract first sentence ↔ highlights bullet 1 ↔ cover letter
  hook must tell the same story (same three axis tokens).

## Group lenses (one agent each, then internal vote)
A. scope-editor lens (TTL-1/3/6, reads SRC-25/26/27 live),
B. search-engine lens (TTL-2/7, reads SRC-08/19 live),
C. overclaim-auditor lens (TTL-4/5/8, applies checklists/07).
Output: 2–4 alternative titles max, each with kept/dropped tokens table + axis_link; the arena
bracket resolves conflicts (SKILL.md §2).
